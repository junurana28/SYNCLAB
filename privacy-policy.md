This Privacy Policy explains how Junu Rana Magar, operating on behalf of the SYNC Lab at Washington University in St. Louis, collects, uses, and protects information including mobile phone numbers in connection with SMS text message communications sent to research study participants.

## Information We Collect

When you agree to receive text messages from us, we collect your mobile phone number and any information you voluntarily provide in connection with your participation in our research study (such as your name, in order to identify you as a study participant). We may also retain a record of your consent to receive text messages and the content of message exchanges related to study reminders and scheduling.

## How We Use Your Information

- To send you SMS, text message reminders and communications related to your participation in the research study

## Message Frequency

Message frequency varies depending on your study participation schedule. Message and data rates may apply.

## Information Sharing

> No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your mobile phone number or text messaging data to any third party. Information collected may be shared only as necessary with Washington University in St. Louis research personnel directly involved in this study, or as required by law or institutional review board (IRB) oversight.

## Data Retention and Security

Your information is stored securely and retained only as long as necessary for the purposes of the research study and in accordance with Washington University in St. Louis institutional data retention and IRB requirements.
