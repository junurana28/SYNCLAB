## Program Description

Junu Rana Magar, operating on behalf of the SYNC Lab at Washington University in St. Louis, offers an SMS text messaging program to send reminders and communications to individuals enrolled as participants in an approved research study. By opting in, you consent to receive text messages related to your study participation, including appointment and task reminders.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Message Frequency** | Varies by study schedule; recurring messages may be sent throughout your participation period. |
| **Cost** | Message and data rates may apply. Charges depend on your mobile carrier and plan. |
| **Opt-Out** | Reply **STOP** at any time to cancel. You will receive a one-time confirmation that no further messages will be sent. |
| **Help** | Reply **HELP** for assistance, or contact us at j.ranamagar@wustl.edu. |

## Eligibility

This messaging program is available only to individuals who have consented to participate in the applicable research study and have agreed to receive text messages as part of that participation.
